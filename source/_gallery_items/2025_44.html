Attempt at a character that's been rolling around in my head for the past few weeks, She would be an antagonist at first but come around and join the protagonists' side at the turning point of the story. Being a kangaroo her main weapon would be kicks but she also has a grappling hook gun she uses to pull foes closer to her (so she can kick them).

The second image is technically week 43 but I didn't want to put it up by itself. Usually I make a page somewhat resembling that for all character designs when I'm trying to figure out what they should look like. Not pretty at all but I figure at least some of you might find that sort of thing interesting.
